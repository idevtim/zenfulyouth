# Support & FAQ

## Get Help

If you have a question, found a bug, or want to request a feature, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/idevtim/zenfulyouth/issues).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who is Zenful Youth for?

Everyone. The "youth" in the name isn't about age: it's the lighter, curious, younger-at-heart you that's still in there, whatever your age. Zenful Youth is a few quiet minutes to find your way back to it.

### How does Zenful Youth work?

Tell it what's on your mind, by typing, speaking, or picking a topic, and choose a length. Zenful Youth writes a new guided meditation about it, with pauses and breathing cues, then guides you through it over a background sound like rain or ocean.

### What do I need to use it?

Meditations are written with Apple Intelligence, so you need an iPhone or iPad that supports Apple Intelligence, running iOS 27 or later, with Apple Intelligence turned on in **Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri**. If it's off or still downloading, the app tells you what to do.

### Why do I need an ElevenLabs key for a voice?

Spoken meditations use voices from [ElevenLabs](https://elevenlabs.io). You connect your own ElevenLabs account with a key, and the app walks you through it step by step. ElevenLabs sets its own age requirements for account holders.

You don't need a key to use the app. Without one, every meditation is shown as gentle on-screen words over your background sound.

### "That key didn't work." What now?

Check that **Text to Speech** is turned on for the key in your ElevenLabs settings, then copy it again and paste it in **Settings › Voice**.

### My voice stopped working partway through the month.

Your ElevenLabs plan has a monthly limit. When it runs out, the app says so, and you can keep going with on-screen words until it resets, or upgrade your ElevenLabs plan.

### Can I listen with my screen locked?

Yes. Sessions keep playing in the background, with controls on the Lock Screen and a Live Activity showing how long is left.

### Why did the background sound stop when I paused?

When a session is paused and you minimise the player or leave the app, the background sound goes quiet too, so nothing keeps playing while you're away. It comes back when you open the player again or press play.

### Can I change the background sound on a replay?

Yes. While a session replays, tap the label at the top of the player (it shows the sound's name) and pick another. The session remembers it for next time.

### Does it work on iPhone Duo?

Yes. Opened up, Zenful Youth uses both sides of the fold: the welcome, Meditate, Sessions and Settings each show two columns.

### Can I stop and finish later?

Yes. Tap the bookmark while a session is playing, or choose to save it when you end early. It waits in **Sessions** and picks up where you left off.

### Does Zenful Youth collect my data?

No. There are no accounts, analytics, or ads, and your sessions, moods, and reflections stay on your device. If you connect a voice, the meditation text is sent to ElevenLabs to be read aloud. See our [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) for details.

### How do I delete my data?

Delete sessions from **Sessions**, disconnect your voice in **Settings › Voice**, or delete the app to remove everything.
